Mission context & controls

The twin launches from Abdul Kalam Island on a 192° azimuth toward its 550 km target orbit. Tracks include the booster, orbital lab, relays, ground stations and return capsule.

Ground tracks are schematic: the exporter places the twin's trajectories along a spherical launch-azimuth path. This view is for mission context, not precision orbit analysis.

Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Select a vehicle to follow it while its trajectory is available. Use the timeline below to seek.
